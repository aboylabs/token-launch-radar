How the model works

implied value = TVL × ref multiple × 0.6 × growth × maturity × chain

  • Ref multiple — the median market-cap / TVL of protocols that already trade, per category. A Lending protocol is priced like listed Lending protocols, not like a DEX. Categories with no listed peers fall back to the global median. The multiple is clamped to 0.05–2.5.
  • Pre-token discount 0.6 — a token that does not exist yet is worth less than the same cash flows already trading: the model takes 60% of the listed-comparable value.
  • Growth — 1 + clamp(7-day TVL change, −50%, +150%) ÷ 100 × 0.6. Fast-growing TVL lifts the value; shrinking TVL cuts it. No 7-day figure counts as 0%.
  • Maturity — 0.7 + min(age in days, 1095) ÷ 1095 × 0.3. A protocol tracked for three years scores 1.0; a brand-new one scores 0.7. Unknown age counts as 365 days.
  • Chain — 1 + 0.04 × min(extra chains beyond the first, 5). Multi-chain deployment adds up to 20%.
  • Eligibility — the board only ranks protocols that could launch a token. Products that are by definition a wrapped, staked or bridged representation of another asset (bridges, liquid staking, wrapped BTC) are not ranked — the same exclusion the sealed watchlist applies. The 40 watchlist protocols are always ranked.

Calibration uses only this snapshot’s own figures: TVL, 7-day change, listing age and the listed market’s multiples. No protocol name is special-cased.
